AAlign V3.0

Welcome

Life, a little easier to hold.

Align is an ADHD-informed organiser designed to reduce the mental effort of keeping day-to-day life together.

🧠 Less mental load

Work, appointments, personal plans and the things you need to remember can live together instead of being scattered across different apps.

🧭 What matters now?

The aim is to help turn “I have too much to do” into a clearer view of what is happening now and what the next manageable step is.

🎨 Make it yours

Add your own sections, names, icons and colours. Your setup can look different from every other person’s.

🍎 Calendar + offline

Link your own iCloud calendars if you want, and keep supported planner information available when your phone is temporarily offline.
