Terms & Privacy Notice

A plain-English summary for this version.

Your personal data

Your planner, shifts, appointments and personal sections are stored in a database allocated to your account. The Align Owner/Admin interface is designed to manage accounts, the default app structure and feedback; it does not provide a screen for browsing your planner content.

Important technical limitation

Align is not zero-access or end-to-end encrypted storage. The person operating the Align server controls the underlying computer, backups and database files and may therefore have technical access at infrastructure level, for example for maintenance, security, recovery or legal obligations.

iCloud Calendar

If you connect iCloud, use an Apple app-specific password, never your normal Apple Account password. Align encrypts the credential in the browser before transmission and encrypts it again while stored on the Align server. You can revoke the app-specific password through Apple.

Testing software

This is the current version of Align. Features may change, syncing may fail and you should keep important information backed up in the original service where appropriate. Keep a copy of anything important outside Align.

Feedback

Feedback you deliberately submit can be viewed by the owner. Align does not automatically attach your planner/calendar content to a feedback report.

This notice is a product disclosure, not formal legal advice. A production privacy policy and terms will need legal review before a wider launch.
