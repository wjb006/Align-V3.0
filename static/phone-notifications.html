Phone notifications

Reminders, even when Align is closed

On iPhone, first open Align in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it using that Home Screen icon. Requires iOS 16.4 or later.

Tap Enable below and allow notifications. This is separate from the Scriptable widget.

Time to leave

When a saved journey has a leave time, Align can notify this phone. A reported bus or train delay moves the alert later. Rain or a storm, using your weather settings, moves it earlier. The message names the event and does not include your address.

What is included

Saved personal reminders, leave-now alerts you opt into, and existing work alerts.

Ordinary reminders stay generic on the lock screen. A leave-now alert says which event it is for, without the address. Open Align for the full journey.

The scheduler checks every minute once activated. On iPhone this works from the installed Home Screen app, not from a Safari tab. Network outages, Focus settings and hosting restarts may delay alerts.
