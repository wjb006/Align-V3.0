Reminders, even when Align is closed

On iPhone, first open Align in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it using that Home Screen icon. Requires iOS 16.4 or later.

Tap Enable below and allow notifications. This is separate from the Scriptable widget.

What is included

Saved personal reminders and existing work alerts. Other appointment and category reminder features are still to be added.

Lock-screen messages hide your reminder details. Open Align to read them.

The scheduler checks every minute once activated. Network outages, Focus settings and free-hosting restarts may delay alerts. Do not rely on these as your only time-critical or medication reminder.
