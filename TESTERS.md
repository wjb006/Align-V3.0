# Trying Align

Align at https://www.getalign.co.uk is in beta. Each person who accepts an invite gets a separate account. Your events, shifts, travel and settings stay in your account. Someone else signed in on the same phone, after you sign out, does not see them.

## Get an invite

Ask Will for an invite. He sends it from Management Studio to your email address. The message contains a one-time code. The code expires after 7 days and works once.

## Create your account

1. Open https://www.getalign.co.uk/invite on your phone.
2. Enter the email address the invite was sent to, the code, and a password.
3. The password needs 10 or more characters, a letter and a number, and must not be a password already published in a breach list.
4. Accept the terms. You then land in your own empty Align.

If you already created the password, use https://www.getalign.co.uk/login. Forgot password is on that page.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the site in Safari. Chrome on iPhone will not install it.
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
3. Open Align from the new icon, not from the Safari tab. It should open full screen, with the dark splash while it starts.
4. Notifications, including “time to leave”, only work from that Home Screen icon, on iOS 16.4 or later. In the app, open Phone notifications, tap Enable, and turn on Tell me when to leave if you want it.

## Install on Android

1. Open the site in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. If a banner offers Install Align, use that.
3. Open Align from the icon. You can then allow notifications from Phone notifications.

## While you are trying it

- Report a problem from your account: open Settings (or the account card) and choose Send feedback. Pick Problem, describe what happened, and send. The message includes the build number and the screen name. It does not attach your calendar.
- The phone keeps today’s plan, the next three days, saved travel notes and the last weather forecast after you have opened Align once while online. The header shows Offline when there is no signal. Edits made offline sync when you are back online.
- Travel uses live stop and timetable data only when those feeds are configured. If a feed is missing, Align says so instead of inventing a bus or a walking time.
