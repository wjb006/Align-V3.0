Align Build 156 · V2.1

- Enables two-way iCloud Calendar sync for Align-owned shifts and section events:
  iCloud edits are pulled back into Align when Align has not also changed the item.
- Copies leave-at journey alerts into iCloud calendar alerts as
  Leave at HH:MM for "Event", and formats travel details in calendar notes.
- Adds a shared alert offset dropdown (5–45 minutes, 1–2 hours, 1–5 days, 1 week)
  for shift alerts and travel reminders.
- Management Studio can edit Home section titles, visibility, order, and bottom
  navigation labels/icons (Pages & text), including Upcoming Events.
- Home Upcoming Events lists items under a shared date heading instead of
  repeating the date on every card.
- Defaults and upgrades rename “Upcoming shifts” to “Upcoming Events”.
